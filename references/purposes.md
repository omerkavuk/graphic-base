# Purposes: defaults and checks by kind of piece

Read the section of the purpose in `STATE.md`. Each adds to the hard rules; none replaces them.

## Brand and marketing

Posts, carousels, stories, web banners, ads, launch visuals, covers.

- **Inputs** (asked in one plain line): the brand name, two or three things that make it different, logo (SVG or a
  large PNG with a transparent ground), brand colours and fonts if they exist, the call to action, the offer and its
  terms (price, dates, conditions).
- **The specificity test (at the card):** write 6–10 facts only this brand has (its product's shape, its place, its
  people, its numbers, its history). The headline or the image uses at least one. Swap test: put a competitor's logo on
  the draft; if it still works, the piece is generic: redo the idea.
- **Logo:** placed as supplied, never redrawn, with clear space of at least its cap height; at least 24 px tall on a
  phone-seen view, 8 mm in print.
- **CTA:** one per piece, a verb and an object, in the third reading level; on a story clear of the bottom 20 %.
- **Legal lines** (offer terms, dates, "images for illustration only"): `data-role="legal"`, at the legal floor or above.
- **Sizes ★:** Instagram post 1080×1350 (4:5) as master; story 1080×1920; square 1080×1080; LinkedIn post 1200×1200
  or 1200×627; X post 1600×900; web banners 1200×628, 970×250, 728×90, 300×250. YouTube thumbnail 1280×720.
- **People:** a recognisable person needs a release: the user's own material or stock that states it (`sources.md` §1).

## Editorial and information

Infographics, one-pagers, report covers and pages, data posts, explainers, educational sheets.

- **Facts first:** every number and claim in `research/facts.md` with its source and year (`sources.md` §4); the source
  line on the piece in its legal level.
- **One message per piece:** the takeaway is the headline, written as a statement, not a topic ("Cities use 40 %
  more water than ten years ago", not "Water use").
- **Charts:** the chart type follows the question (change over time: a line; parts of a whole: a single stacked bar,
  rarely a pie; comparison: bars sorted by value; place: a map from Natural Earth shapes). Direct labels instead of
  legends; the one series that matters in the accent, the rest in a muted role; no 3D, no gridline clutter; the axis
  starts at zero for bars.
- **Reading order:** a clear path (top to bottom, left to right, or numbered); no more than seven chunks on one page.
- **Sizes ★:** A4 portrait (210×297 mm) for print and PDF; 1080×1350 for a data post; a carousel of 1080×1350 slides
  for a step-by-step explainer.
- **Reports** (several pages): Studio mode (`studio-mode.md`): a page plan with a kind per page, the copy in full
  before any page, a style frame, the layout sheet, then the draft. Print booklets in multiples of 4 pages.

## Event and personal

Invitations, event posters, save-the-dates, cards, menus, programmes, certificates.

- **The facts are the content:** who invites, what, when (day, date, time), where (name and address), how to reply
  (by when, to whom), dress or notes if any. Ask for them exactly; never invent one; check the weekday matches the date.
- **Tone from the occasion:** a wedding, a launch, a birthday, a memorial, a conference each set the type and colour;
  the card names the tone in three words and the face carries it.
- **Print by default:** invitations and cards are designed for print (unit mm, 3 mm bleed, 300 dpi) and also exported
  as a PNG for messaging; posters for print at A3 or A2 and a phone-seen PNG.
- **Sizes ★:** invitation A6 (105×148 mm) or 5×7 in (127×178 mm); card A6 or DL (99×210 mm); poster A3 (297×420 mm)
  or A2 (420×594 mm); menu A4 or DL; certificate A4 landscape; save-the-date 1080×1350 for messaging.
- **Paper:** say in one line what paper the design expects (uncoated warm white for a hand-made feel, coated for photos)
  so the user can tell the printer; the design's ground colour simulates it only when the card says so.
