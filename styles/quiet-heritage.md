# Quiet Heritage

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): heritage and craft brand sites (O.A. Devold): an olive ground, an elegant serif, a calm nature photograph in a split layout.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **Split page:** text on a coloured half, a photograph on the other.
- **An unhurried serif,** medium size, sentence case, generous leading.
- **Nature and making:** landscapes, materials, hands at work; never staged.

Not rustic kitsch (no wood textures, no stamps), not luxury gold.

## 2. Materials and rendering
- Ground from the earth: olive #6e6a48, clay, moss, sand; photograph with natural light.

## 3. Colour logic
- Ground colour, cream text (#f3efe4), photo colours; no bright accent.

## 4. Type
- A refined serif (Cormorant, EB Garamond, Fraunces at low optical contrast, Newsreader); a small sans for labels.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Cream on olive must still reach 4.5:1 for text.
- Photograph must be calm: one subject, lots of air.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: heritage and craft brands, food and land, sustainability stories, nonprofit reports, invitations.
- Take the grammar (palette logic, type, spacing, structure), never the reference's layout.
