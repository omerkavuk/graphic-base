# Black Condensed

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): beverage and product brand sites (Darkroom): a black ground, giant cream condensed capitals, one grey product photograph.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **Condensed capitals fill the width,** cream on black, tight leading.
- **One object, studio-lit,** in greys, placed under or over the word.
- **Small precise details:** tiny labels, thin rules, a small panel of options as a graphic accent.

Not grunge (clean edges), not luxury gold (no metallics).

## 2. Materials and rendering
- Ground #0d0d0d; type #f1ede4; the photo desaturated to greys with soft shadow.

## 3. Colour logic
- Black, cream, greys; one tiny accent (a violet or red square) if needed.

## 4. Type
- A heavy condensed grotesque (Anton, Oswald 700, Big Shoulders Display, League Gothic) for the word; a small mono or grotesque for labels.

## 5. Pitfalls
- The word too long to stay huge: one or two words.
- Photo too dark against black: lift it with a grey ground or rim light.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: product launches, drinks and food brands, music and nightlife posters, sports, bold campaign banners.
- Take the grammar (palette logic, type, spacing, structure), never the reference's layout.
