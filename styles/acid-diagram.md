# Acid Diagram

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): infrastructure software sites (Mezmo): a deep green ground, heavy lime capitals, a thin-line system diagram.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **A loud statement** in heavy condensed capitals, stacked left, lime on dark green.
- **A diagram as the picture:** rounded boxes, arrows and simple line icons in lime, explaining a flow.
- **Technical labels:** monospace capitals, small, with dots or pills.

Not a dashboard screenshot, not neon cyberpunk (no glow).

## 2. Materials and rendering
- Ground #0e2a14 to #10260f; line work 1.5 px lime; boxes with small radius.

## 3. Colour logic
- Dark green ground, lime #d8f36a for type and lines, white for small text.

## 4. Type
- Heavy condensed grotesque (Anton, Archivo Black condensed, Big Shoulders Display 800); a monospace (IBM Plex Mono, Space Mono) for labels.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Diagram too dense: five to eight boxes.
- Lime body text on green is tiring: keep it for display.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: explainers of a process or system, tech and data reports, campaign stats, carousel slides, conference banners.
- Take the grammar (palette logic, type, spacing, structure), never the reference's layout.
