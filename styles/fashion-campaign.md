# Fashion Campaign

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): fashion and artist-management sites (Vivien's Creative): a muted full-bleed photograph and a giant white wordmark cropped at the edge.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **The photograph is the page:** soft daylight, muted greys and naturals, a person or a scene with mood.
- **A wordmark too big for the page:** heavy sans capitals in white, running off the bottom or side edge.
- **One quiet sentence:** a centred serif line at the top, small navigation-like labels in the corners.

Not a sale banner (no badges, no prices on the image), not an over-saturated stock photo.

## 2. Materials and rendering
- The photograph graded down: lower saturation, lifted blacks, a cool or warm cast.
- The giant word cropped by 15 to 30 % of its height; corner labels in small caps.

## 3. Colour logic
- Colours come from the photograph; type in white (or black on a very light photo).

## 4. Type
- Giant word in a heavy grotesque (Archivo Black, Anton for a condensed version, Inter Display Black); the sentence in a refined serif (Cormorant, EB Garamond, Newsreader).

## 5. Pitfalls
- The cropped word must still read: crop the bottom, not the middle.
- Faces in brand work need a release (`sources.md` §1).

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: campaign posts and banners, fashion and beauty, culture and festival posters, people stories, covers.
- Take the grammar (palette logic, type, spacing, structure), never the reference's layout.
