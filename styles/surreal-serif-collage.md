# Surreal Serif Collage

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): type foundry specimen sites (JULY01 by Studio K95): a huge thin display serif, a black and white cut-out photograph, one red-orange bar.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **The serif is huge and light:** hairline contrast, set wide across the top, letters spaced.
- **An odd, real picture:** an archive photograph cut out cleanly (an astronaut reading a newspaper), overlapping the letters.
- **One hot colour:** a red-orange band or a single dot.

Not a magazine cover (no cover lines), not a collage of many pieces: one picture.

## 2. Materials and rendering
- White ground; the cut-out in black and white with crisp edges; the bar full width at the top or bottom.

## 3. Colour logic
- White, black, greys; one red-orange #e8461f.

## 4. Type
- A display serif with extreme contrast (Bodoni Moda, Playfair Display at light weights, Italiana, Gloock); a small sans for details.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Archive photos need an open licence (`sources.md` §1).
- Thin serifs vanish small: only at display size.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: culture and art events, talks, editorial covers, campaign posts with an idea, book and music announcements.
- Take the grammar (palette logic, type, spacing, structure), never the reference's layout.
