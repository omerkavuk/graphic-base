# Memphis

> The Milan design collective of the 1980s. References (grammar only): Ettore Sottsass, Nathalie Du Pasquier's
> patterns, Peter Shire's forms.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not

- **Pattern as a material:** squiggles, confetti dots, terrazzo, zigzags, grids, stripes; shapes as if cut from
  laminate.
- **Geometric solids stacked off-balance:** a cylinder on a cone on a cube, a half circle leaning on a bar.
- **Clashing colour on purpose,** held together by black outlines or black pattern.
- Not "random shapes everywhere": a few big forms, patterns inside them, a clear place for the words.

## 2. Materials and rendering

- Flat SVG shapes with thick black strokes or none; patterns built as SVG `<pattern>` fills with a fixed seed.
- No gradients, no shadows except a hard offset block shadow when the card names it.

## 3. Colour logic

- Pastels (mint, pink, lilac, butter yellow) with saturated accents (red, cobalt, black); 4–6 colours, each with a job.

## 4. Type

- Bold geometric or quirky display faces; mixed case; type may sit inside a shape. Body text plain and readable.

## 5. Pitfalls

- Every corner filled; text placed over busy pattern (contrast falls); the 2010s "Memphis revival" clichés with
  sparkles and gradients.

## 6. Variation space

- Pattern-led (one pattern full bleed, a white box for type) · object-led (a stacked sculpture as the hero) · muted
  Memphis (two pastels and black) · typographic (letters as laminate shapes).
