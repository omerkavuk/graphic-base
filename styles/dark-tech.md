# Dark Tech

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): contemporary product and founder sites in dark mode (the Footer gallery, The Uncommon Founder): near-black, one soft neon accent, quiet cards.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **Dark, not loud:** the page is black or graphite; only one word or one shape carries the accent colour.
- **Product precision:** a clean grotesque, small labels, thin hairline rules, rounded pill buttons.
- **A field of small marks:** scattered squares or dots like data points give texture without a picture.

Not cyberpunk (no glow, no gradients, no neon grids), not the purple AI gradient look.

## 2. Materials and rendering
- Ground #0f0f10 to #1a1a1a; panels one step lighter (#202022) with 1 px borders at 8 % white.
- Corner radius 10 to 16 px on cards and pills, consistent everywhere.
- Data marks: small squares 4 to 10 px in white at 30 to 90 % opacity, placed on a loose grid.

## 3. Colour logic
- White and two greys for text (#f2f2f2, #a0a0a0, #6b6b6b); one accent: soft lime #e4f98a, mint #b6f5d0 or electric yellow #f4ff61, used on one word or one element.

## 4. Type
- One grotesque in two weights (Geist, Inter Tight, Manrope, Satoshi by reason, not habit: `craft.md` §6); headline light or regular, large; labels small with generous tracking.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Grey text too dim: keep body at 4.5:1 on the dark ground.
- Accent on several things: it stops signalling.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: tech launches, founder and startup posts, event banners for conferences, product announcements, data highlights.
- Take the grammar (palette logic, type, spacing, structure), never the reference's layout.
