# Duotone Photography

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): photo-led campaign posters and record sleeves that print one photograph in two inks.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **One strong photograph** (open archive or the user's) printed in two colours: its shadows in a dark ink, its lights in
  a bright one.
- **Bold type** placed in the calm part of the image, or on a solid band.

Not a stock-photo-with-text-overlay template (the photo is treated, cropped hard, and owns the page).

## 2. Materials and rendering
- Convert the photo to greyscale, map it to the two inks (a CSS or SVG filter: `feColorMatrix` then `feComponentTransfer`),
  crop it hard; an optional coarse halftone.

## 3. Colour logic
- Two inks that hold contrast (navy and coral, black and yellow, green and pink); type in the lighter ink or white.

## 4. Type
- A heavy grotesque or condensed sans, large; details small on a solid band.

## 5. Pitfalls
- A weak photo: the style depends on it; choose by the image's shape and light first.
- Faces in a brand piece need a release (`sources.md` §1).

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: campaigns, event posters, people stories, music, nonprofit appeals.
