# Punk Zine Collage

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): punk and DIY zines (photocopied cut-and-paste, ransom-note letters, tape, staples).

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **Cut and paste:** photocopied photographs, torn paper, tape, letters cut from different sources, handwritten notes.
- **Black and white photocopy** texture with one fluorescent or red accent.

Not the paper explainer collage (rougher, louder, less ordered), not grunge texture over clean design.

## 2. Materials and rendering
- High-contrast photocopy treatment on photographs (threshold, toner noise), torn edges, tape strips, staple marks.

## 3. Colour logic
- Black, white, grey toner and one accent (fluorescent pink, yellow or red).

## 4. Type
- Mixed cut-out letters for one headline; a typewriter or marker hand for notes; never more than one ransom headline.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Unreadable details; real people's photos without rights; too much texture.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: music and youth events, activism and campaigns, zines, alternative brands.
