# Minimal Corporate

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): contemporary identity systems of public institutions and consultancies; annual
reports that trust white space.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **Calm authority:** one neutral ground, one brand colour used sparingly, generous white space, a precise grid.
- **One clear message per page:** a short headline, one supporting fact, the logo.
- **Restraint is the signature:** few elements, perfectly aligned; a single thin rule or a coloured block as the accent.

Not the AI-average look (no gradients, glass cards, floating UI or stock people at laptops), not Swiss poster drama (less
scale contrast, quieter).

## 2. Materials and rendering
- Flat surfaces only; a 12-column grid with wide margins (8–10 % of the short side).
- One graphic device repeated across a series: a corner block, a thin rule, a cropped circle, a single photograph.
- Photography, when used, is documentary and calm: real people at real work, natural light, never posed stock.

## 3. Colour logic
- A near-white or very light warm grey ground, a dark ink, and the brand colour for 5–15 % of the page.
- Without a brand colour: one deep colour from the subject (a forest green, a petrol blue, a brick red).

## 4. Type
- One sober grotesque or humanist sans in two or three weights (check `craft.md` §6: not Inter, Roboto, Montserrat or
  Poppins by habit); numbers in tabular figures; headline 3–4× the body.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Too empty reads as unfinished: one strong element must lead.
- Brand colour everywhere: it stops signalling.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: corporate posts, reports, conference banners, institutional announcements, job posts.
