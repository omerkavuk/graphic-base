# Big Type Index

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): studio and agency index pages (Porto Rocha's Developments): huge, tight grotesque headlines, numbered entries, a hand-drawn mark.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **One giant word block** fills the width, set tight (tracking −2 to −4 %, leading 0.85).
- **An index rhythm:** a big number with a full stop above the headline, a thin rule between entries, small caps details below.
- **One human mark:** a hand-drawn circle, a Venn of two words, a scribbled underline over the strict type.

Not Swiss (no strict grid, no red), not brutalist (polished, not raw).

## 2. Materials and rendering
- White or off-white ground, black ink; the hand-drawn mark in black line or the single accent.
- Pill-shaped outline labels (1 px, fully rounded) for categories, stacked at a margin.

## 3. Colour logic
- Black on white by default; one accent at most (a red dot, a blue scribble).

## 4. Type
- A neo-grotesque with tight fit at display size (Neue Haas-like: Inter Display, Schibsted Grotesk, Hanken Grotesk, Instrument Sans); a condensed or italic companion for the second line; small caps for details.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Headline too long to set big: three words or fewer per line.
- Hand mark too neat: it should feel drawn, slightly uneven.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: announcements, event and talk posters, agency and studio posts, report covers, quotes set large.
- Take the grammar (palette logic, type, spacing, structure), never the reference's layout.
