# Photo Index

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): creative-house and studio sites (Phamilia): a saturated close-up photograph filling the page, white sans names laid over it like an index.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **A close crop with energy:** a face, a hand, food, a texture, in full colour.
- **Type as an index over the picture:** several bold white words in a row, each with a tiny label before it.
- **Small dense text** in a corner as texture.

Not a quote over a photo (more than one word level), not a dark overlay.

## 2. Materials and rendering
- The photograph sharp and saturated; text directly on it where the photo is calm enough.

## 3. Colour logic
- From the photograph; type white (or black on light areas).

## 4. Type
- A bold grotesque (Inter Display, Schibsted Grotesk, Bricolage Grotesque) at two sizes; tiny labels in the same face.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Text over busy areas: place it on calm skin, sky or shadow; check contrast.
- Faces in brand work need a release.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: campaigns, culture and festival line-ups, people stories, food and beauty, covers and carousels.
- Take the grammar (palette logic, type, spacing, structure), never the reference's layout.
