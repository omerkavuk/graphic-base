# Brutalist

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): brutalist web design and contemporary anti-design posters: raw grids, system fonts,
hard contrast, visible structure.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **Raw and direct:** big type at hard sizes, visible grid lines and boxes, no decoration, no softness.
- **Structure shown, not hidden:** borders, labels, numbers, the scaffolding of the layout as the aesthetic.
- High contrast, often black and white with one loud colour.

Not careless (the rawness is precise), not expressive type (fewer distortions, more structure).

## 2. Materials and rendering
- Thick borders, boxes, ruled tables, monospace labels; images cropped hard or none.

## 3. Colour logic
- Black, white, and one loud colour (signal yellow, electric blue, hot red).

## 4. Type
- A heavy grotesque or a monospace at very large sizes; tight or default spacing on purpose.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Hostile to read: hierarchy still leads the eye; one clear first read.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: tech and design events, manifestos, launches, bold campaigns, youth brands.
