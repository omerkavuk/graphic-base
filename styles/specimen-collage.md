# Specimen Collage

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): type and letterform journals (Counter Forms): pastel cards scattered like pinned notes, each a type specimen.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **Cards, not a grid:** rectangles in soft yellow, sky blue, sage and sand, overlapping a little, placed by eye.
- **Each card shows a letter or a word big,** in its own face, with a caption naming it.
- **Reading texture:** some cards hold a short paragraph in a serif.

Not Memphis (no squiggles, no confetti), not a moodboard of photos.

## 2. Materials and rendering
- White or off-white ground; cards flat, no shadows; small overlaps.
- Monospace captions in capitals top-left of each card.

## 3. Colour logic
- Pastels: #ffe14d yellow, #b8d3ea blue, #cfc79a sand, #c9d3c3 sage; black type.

## 4. Type
- Three or four contrasting display faces (a blackletter or wedge serif, an italic, a book serif, a grotesque), one monospace for captions.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Too many cards: five to seven.
- Faces must have the language's letters (`craft.md`).

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: culture and design events, festival programmes, editorial covers, quote collections, carousels.
- Take the grammar (palette logic, type, spacing, structure), never the reference's layout.
