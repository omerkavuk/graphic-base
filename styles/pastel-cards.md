# Pastel Cards

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): friendly startup and team sites (Huddle): a warm grey ground, a big plain-sans promise, colourful rounded project cards with tags.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **A promise in one sentence,** large, in a plain sans, left aligned.
- **Cards carry the content:** each card one item with a status label, a two-line title and two or three tag pills.
- **A human touch:** small round avatars inline in the headline, or one rounded black button.

Not generic SaaS (no gradients, no floating 3D blobs, no stock laptops).

## 2. Materials and rendering
- Ground #e4e4df; cards 16 to 24 px radius, flat colour, no shadow; tag pills darker tone of the card.

## 3. Colour logic
- Card palette: #c9bfe6 lilac, #e1a3a8 rose, #e8b866 mustard, #aebfbd sage; dark ink #1b1b1b.

## 4. Type
- One friendly grotesque (Hanken Grotesk, DM Sans by reason, General Sans, Instrument Sans) in two weights; tags in small capitals.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Too many cards per page: three to five.
- Pastel text on pastel: titles in dark ink.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: team and hiring posts, programme and project overviews, nonprofit results, product feature posts, carousels.
- Take the grammar (palette logic, type, spacing, structure), never the reference's layout.
