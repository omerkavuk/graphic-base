# Newspaper Broadsheet

> graphic base's own design recipe. References (grammar only): broadsheet front pages, newsroom typography, wire-service tables.

## 1. Essence, and what it is not
- **A front page:** a masthead or kicker bar, a huge headline across columns, dense columns of text, rules everywhere.
- **Information density** as the look: datelines, bylines, small captions, a table or a big number.
- One strong photograph or chart, cropped hard.

Not a tabloid parody, not editorial magazine (denser, more columns, less white space).

## 2. Materials and rendering
- Newsprint ground (warm light grey), black ink; hairline column rules and heavy section rules.
- Photographs in black and white or a single duotone with a coarse halftone where it fits.

## 3. Colour logic
- Newsprint, black, and one spot colour at most (a red for the masthead or the key number).

## 4. Type
- A condensed bold serif or grotesque for headlines; a readable text serif for columns; small caps for datelines.

## 5. Pitfalls
- Fake lorem-style filler: every line on the page is real.
- Too many headlines of the same size: one leads, the rest step down.

## 6. Variation space
- Formats: explainers, reports, "breaking" announcements, press releases, data posts, carousels of findings.
