# Style pictures

One picture per style, shown on the style board to choose a look. Mood only: never placed in a design, never copied.

**Made with graphic base** (fictional sample pieces): bauhaus, botanical-plate, brutalist, cartographic, duotone-photo,
editorial-magazine, halftone-dossier, memphis, mid-century-modern, minimal-corporate, newspaper, punk-zine,
vox-explainer. Material in them: Vincent van Gogh, *The Poet's Garden* (Art Institute of Chicago, CC0); an *Amaryllis*
botanical plate (rawpixel via Openverse, CC0); open fonts (SIL OFL 1.1, Apache 2.0).

**From the lemo-opuscar style library** by LemoLab (MIT, `styles/LICENSE-lemo-opuscar`): briefing-board, dataviz,
engraving, iso-infographic, risograph, swiss, watercolor, woodcut.
